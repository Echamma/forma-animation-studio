# Forma Animation Studio — Desktop and MCP 1.2.0

**A friendlier editor.** Forma has a new look and opens on a start screen that asks what you want to make: animate the example shapes, build a stop-motion from a few pictures (Forma lines them up as poses, half a second each), or start from an empty canvas in landscape or portrait. Your saved projects appear as cards below. In the editor, the tools sit in a rail on the left: Select, Shape, Text, Picture, Draw move, Layers, and Scene. Select a layer and a bar under the canvas offers one-click Move, Spin, Fade, and Drop in. The inspector leads with the things you change most (look and easing) and folds exact numbers into **Position & size**. Easing uses plain names: Smooth, Steady, Hold, Speed up, Slow down, or a custom curve. Export asks what to save and shows the length and frame count before you save.

**Stop-motion clips.** Every layer now has a timing bar on the timeline. Drag it to move the layer and its animation in time, or drag either end to trim it; drags snap to the playhead, other clips, and 0.1 s steps (hold Alt to place freely). To make a picture jump from place to place, give a clip an end time, choose **Continue after this clip** (Ctrl+Shift+V), and drag the copy to its next spot. Copies share one timeline row, and onion skin shows the previous pose while you place the next one. Copy a different drawing with Ctrl+C and press Ctrl+Shift+V on a clip to put it next instead. **S** splits a clip at the playhead without changing what is on screen; Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, and Ctrl+D copy, paste, and duplicate.

**Animation.** Right-click a keyframe to choose how it eases into the next one: ease in, ease out, ease in and out, linear, hold, or a custom curve shaped by dragging two handles. **Animate the fill color** turns color changes into color keyframes. Shift-click to select several layers; they move, copy, and delete together, and **Arrange** aligns or spaces them. Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redoes.

**Export.** Video renders every frame at its exact time to MP4 (H.264 with AAC where supported) or WebM, at 24, 30, or 60 fps and 1× or 2× size. It runs faster than real time, works with the window hidden, includes sound from video layers, and can be cancelled. Animated SVG is much smaller and exact at keyframes and clip cuts, and embeds its images. **Project file (.forma)** packs an editable project with its images and video into one file.

**Desktop.** Imported images and videos are stored once in a media library, so large videos no longer sit in memory, undo history, or autosaves; projects from earlier versions move their media there when opened. `.forma` files open from the app or the file manager. The interface fonts are bundled, so the editor and its exports look the same offline. Linked-text controls now appear when a text layer is selected, and timeline keyframes draw as diamonds again.

**MCP.** Thirteen tools. The new `forma_duplicate_shape` places a still copy after a clip, optionally at x/y, to build stop-motion sequences. Shape tools accept clip timing (`inTime`, `outTime`, `trackId`), keyframes accept easing and custom curves, `forma_update_shape` accepts color keyframes, and `forma_export` adds MP4 with a frame rate. Project and SVG exports embed their media. Setup is in `mcp/README.md` on the `linux` branch.

**Downloads.** Windows: `Forma-Animation-Studio-Setup-1.2.0-x64.exe` installs Forma and updates it from new releases; `Forma-Animation-Studio-1.2.0-x64-portable.exe` runs without installing. Neither is code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen asks you to confirm the first launch. Linux: the x86-64 AppImage (updates itself) and the Debian/Ubuntu package. Also `forma-mcp.cjs` for MCP clients (Node.js 22 or later) and SHA-256 checksums.

Verified with unit tests of the animation model, the Electron editor tests (clips and stop-motion frames, easing, color, multi-select, the media library, `.forma` round trips, decoded MP4 and WebM frame counts and audio, and SVG playback), a real MCP stdio client against all thirteen tools, and packaged-app startup. The original browser app remains on `main`; desktop/MCP source is on `linux`.
