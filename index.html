Make your first animation

  1. Add a layer. Use Shape, Text, or Picture on the left.
  2. Set a moment. Drag the playhead in the timeline, then drag, resize, or recolor the layer. Forma remembers it as a keyframe.
  3. Make it move. Use Move, Spin, Fade, or Drop in under the canvas, or set another moment.
  4. Stop-motion. Give a picture an end time, press Next pose, and drag the copy.

Press Ctrl + Z to undo and S to split a clip at the playhead. Press play to preview, then Export.
